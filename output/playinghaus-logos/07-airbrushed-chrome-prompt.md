## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one spectacular original airbrushed chrome Playinghaus logo, inspired by late-1970s custom-van lettering, pinball backglass and airbrushed record sleeves.
Exact text: "playinghaus", all lowercase, spelled p-l-a-y-i-n-g-h-a-u-s, on one continuous highly legible line. No other text.
Typography: substantial flowing custom 1970s script, confident soft curves, gently rising baseline, rounded muscular strokes, elegant joins, open counters. An integrated generous underline swash. Strong silhouette and impeccable optical spacing, every letter readable.
Finish: hand-airbrushed mirror-polished silver chrome, curved sculptural letter faces with sharp white highlights, deep near-black reflected bands, pale icy-blue upper reflections and warm amber/copper sunset reflections along lower edges. Fine luminous silver edging separates the lettering from the dark ground. Rich realistic reflective depth rendered as vintage commercial airbrush illustration, not cheap software bevels. Three restrained pinpoint four-point star glints at key metal edges.
Composition: one centered wide wordmark filling most of a 1536 x 1024 landscape canvas, ample breathing room on all sides, completely visible swash. Plain deep midnight charcoal background, a very subtle warm halo immediately behind the lettering. No scenery, no frame, no extra icons.
Mood: cool, tactile, lovingly painted, late-70s chrome glamour. No neon magenta, no synthwave grid, no modern esports angles, no cars, no cartridges, no mockup, no existing brand imitation, no watermark. This is a standalone new logo concept, not a website screenshot.
