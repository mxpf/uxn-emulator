## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create one new SKETCHPAD cartridge image for the Playinghaus library, matching these two existing cartridge assets as a coherent series. Image 1 is the edit base for exact black cartridge shell, front-facing camera, molded grip, proportions, white background and soft shadow. Image 2 is a supporting series reference for label treatment and cream Constellation branding. Change only the printed paper label: preserve the black shell, its realistic worn texture, outline, camera, scale, margins, neutral white background and lighting. Portrait aspect ratio 4:5, complete cartridge visible, same framing as reference assets. New label design: rich muted petrol-teal field, lightly distressed paper, warm cream lettering. At the top reproduce the existing cream Constellation emblem and exact word 'CONSTELLATION'. Below use very large bold late-1970s rounded geometric typography reading exactly 'SKETCHPAD', one word, split across two lines as 'SKETCH' and 'PAD' if necessary for the bold series layout. No other wording. Illustration in the cream-bordered lower panel: a beautifully rendered amber-yellow wooden drawing pencil, large and diagonally placed, its graphite point in contact with a cream sheet of subtly gridded drawing paper; the graphite line becomes a charming angular dark-navy pixel staircase and a few simple blocky doodles. An understated pink rubber eraser on the paper hints at the second tool. Nostalgic 1970s airbrushed and hand-painted educational-computer cover art, warm light, tactile printed halftone texture, confident simple composition, clear drawing theme at thumbnail size. Palette coordinates with the sketchpad interface: cream, navy, amber, with petrol-teal label. Illustration must stay entirely within the label, never alter the shell. No modern UI, no buttons, no extra badges, no people, no extraneous objects, no Playinghaus wordmark on the cartridge, no watermark. Deliver a polished full-cartridge asset on pure white suitable for the existing minimal website.
